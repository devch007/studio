🧭 Welcome to ECOSYSTEM 3D Lab (Class 6)

Step 1

Set Initial Populations

Use the sliders, − / + buttons, or drag + Drag Herb / Hare / Wolf tokens directly onto the 3D grassland.

Step 2

Press Start & Control Speed

Click Start at the bottom to begin time. Switch between 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 4× or use Step while paused.

Step 3

Watch & Inspect Organisms

Click any herb, hare, or wolf to view live Vitality (red) and Stamina (green), or drag animals to new spots.

Step 4

Run Ecosystem Experiments

Open the Experiments tab to trigger a Drought, remove wolves, or add herbs, then study how the Population Graph responds!

Camera Controls: Drag empty ground to rotate 360° • Right-drag (or two fingers) to pan • Scroll wheel or pinch to zoom • Drag any organism to move it!
