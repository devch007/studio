⚖ Compare: Series Circuit vs Parallel Circuit

Two Bulbs in Series
Two Bulbs in Parallel
Feature Series Circuit Parallel Circuit
Conducting Paths Only one single path for electric current. Two or more separate branches for current.
Bulb Brightness (identical bulbs, ideal battery) Voltage divides — each bulb glows dimmer than a single bulb. Each branch gets full battery voltage — bulbs glow brightly.
If One Bulb Is Removed The single path breaks — the other bulb goes OFF. The other branch stays complete — the other bulb stays ON.
Current Behaviour Same current passes through every bulb in the loop. Current splits at the junction and combines again at return.
