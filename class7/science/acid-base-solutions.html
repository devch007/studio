Hands-On 3D Chemistry Lab Guide

1. Place Glassware

Drag a Glass Beaker from the left LAB EQUIPMENT rack onto the empty dark workbench.

2. Pour Solutions

Open SOLUTIONS, drag an acid, base, or distilled water bottle near the beaker, and click Pour into Beaker.

3. Measure pH & Conductivity

Drag pH Paper, the Digital pH Meter, or the Conductivity Probe onto the beaker to take real readings.

4. Inspect Ions & Neutralise

Activate View Particles to inspect dissociation, or pour NaOH into HCl to observe neutralisation (HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O).
